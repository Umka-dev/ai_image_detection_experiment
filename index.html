About the experiment

Can you tell what is real?

This interactive quiz explores how difficult it can be to distinguish between Real, Digitally Edited, and AI Generated images.

The experiment uses 16 publicly available images of Elon Musk collected from news articles, interviews, books, public events, and online media. Each image was analyzed with TruthScan AI Image Detector and its assessment is used as a reference throughout the quiz.

Before revealing the detector’s result, try to classify each image yourself and compare your judgment with the AI. The goal is not to prove whether an image is authentic, but to demonstrate how challenging visual verification has become as synthetic media continues to improve.

Disclaimer: AI image detectors provide probabilistic assessments and may produce false positives or false negatives. Their results should be treated as supporting evidence rather than definitive proof.
